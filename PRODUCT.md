## Stack

## Users

People in Rio de Janeiro (mostly Zona Oeste / Jacarepaguá) who found Old Seven Tattoo on Instagram and tapped through to the site on their phone. They are deciding whether this studio's work is good enough for their piece, and want the shortest path to sending their idea on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

One-page site for a tattoo studio. It shows the work, says where the studio is, and turns a visit into a WhatsApp conversation. Success = a visitor opens WhatsApp with the prefilled message.

## Positioning

Assumption (inferred from the supplied reels, not confirmed by the user): the studio's portfolio is large-scale black-and-grey realism (pop culture and horror pieces: Naruto calf piece, Jason / Friday the 13th thigh piece, lion half-sleeve) plus cover-ups, delivered from a studio whose identity is old-school American traditional (banner logo with rose).
